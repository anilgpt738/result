About Usha Institute Of Computer Education

Fostering digital literacy, employment skills, and technical excellence in Mau & eastern Uttar Pradesh since establishment.

Our Heritage & Mission

Committed to Practical IT Skills & Quality Education

Usha Institute Of Computer Education (ATC: IN/UP/MAU/29172) is a premier computer educational center located at Hanuman Mandir ke pass, Pahasa Bazar, Mau, Uttar Pradesh. Our objective is to bridge the digital divide by equipping youth with foundational and advanced computer applications, business accounting, and digital productivity skills.

Affiliated under the Vocational and Skill Training framework of The Bright Futures, our curriculum strictly conforms to Skill India & National Skill Development guidelines. All students receive comprehensive hands-on laboratory practice, periodic evaluation, and verifiable certificates.
